# Recipe Costing

<!-- Restaurant skill. Read-only: it reaches the restaurant's data ONLY
through the restaurant's own Bread 'n' Butter MCP (`.mcp.json`; verbs
recipe_cost · list_rows). The costing is the restaurant database's views
(supabase/restaurant/0002_costing.sql in the app repo) — this skill never
computes a cost and never writes one. -->

A recipe's cost is never stored: the database computes it on every read from
the latest invoice prices, the recipe's lines, its prep recipes and its yield.
So nothing goes stale and nothing needs re-costing — an invoice entered a
minute ago is already in every dish above it. This skill's job is to **read
the cost and explain it** in words a cook can check.

## What the database answers

| Where | What it gives |
|---|---|
| `recipe_cost` `{ recipe_id }` | the recipe's `recipe_costs` row — `total_cost`, `cost_per_yield_unit`, `food_cost_pct` (menu dishes), `status` (`costed` · `cost incomplete` · `cost incomplete: loop or too deep`), `incomplete_lines` — and every line from `recipe_line_costs` with `unit_cost`, `line_cost`, `status` and **`working`**, the line's math in words |
| `recipe_cost` `{ recipe_id, as_of: "YYYY-MM-DD" }` | the same, with only the prices known on that date and the recipe as it was that day (its lines, yield and menu price) |
| `list_rows` `recipe_line_versions` / `recipe_versions` | the recipe's history, kept by the database: each old version of a line or of the recipe's yield / menu price, with `valid_from` → `valid_to`. Read-only. Changes on the day a recipe got its first line are its starting recipe and keep no version |
| `list_rows` `recipe_costs` | every recipe at once (filter `is_prep`, sort `-food_cost_pct`) |
| `list_rows` `cost_alerts` | dishes over **30%**, or up **2+ points** since the day before the latest delivery |
| `list_rows` `ingredient_costs` | each ingredient's price per base unit, the date and the vendor it came from |
| `list_rows` `recipe_held_lines` `{ recipe_id }` | `held`: how many card lines are **held** — parked by recipe-entry because their ingredient doesn't exist yet, has no price, or its unit can't convert — on the recipe **and every prep beneath it**, with their `decision_ids` and `titles`. A held line was never written, so the cost above doesn't count it, even when `status` says `costed` |

A prep line's `working` names the prep's batch cost; call `recipe_cost` on its
`sub_recipe_id` to go one level deeper.

## Breakdown preps, yields and by-products

A **breakdown prep** (a prep whose one line is a raw ingredient cut down
before use — Hangar Steak, Trimmed from Hangar Steak) is costed per
**usable** unit, with the price and the yield from the **same delivery**.
`list_rows` `breakdown_costs` gives each one's `method`, `lot`, `yield_pct`,
`label`, `working` and `heads_up`; its line in `recipe_cost` carries the same
working. Three methods, always named:

| Method | When | The cost is |
|---|---|---|
| **matched** | the newest delivery (skipping one still waiting under 3 days) has a yield log | that delivery's price × weight in ÷ weight out: `2.0 lb × $14.0000 per lb (Hangar Steak — US Foods #4471 2026-10-28) ÷ 1.7 lb trimmed (85% — yield log 2026-10-28, same delivery) = $16.4706 per lb` |
| **estimated** | that delivery has waited 3 days or more with no yield log | its price ÷ the **usual yield** (the vendor's last 5 logs; else every vendor's; else the standard): `$14.0000 per lb ÷ 80% usual yield (US Foods, last 1 log) = $17.5000 per lb — estimated` |
| **standard yield** | no yield log of this prep yet | like any prep: its one line ÷ its typed yield |

- **The heads-up line** names a newer delivery still waiting under 3 days
  and what it would cost at the usual yield ("the 10/30 delivery at the usual
  82% would be $17.0732 per lb"). Quote it; it is not the cost yet.
- **A by-product** (a prep that is the trim of a breakdown prep: Hangar Fat
  Trim) costs **$0** per yield unit, and a recipe line on it is $0.0000. The
  delivery's price is counted once, in the main cut; the working lists the
  by-product as `+ 0.3 lb Hangar Fat Trim (by-product, $0)`.
- **Yield alerts.** `list_rows` `yield_alerts`: a yield log more than **10
  points** under the usual yield before it, with `alert` in words ("65%,
  against US Foods' usual 82.5%"). Name it when a dish moved because of it —
  a bad box costs more per usable pound.
- **Usable vs paid.** `yield_by_vendor` gives each vendor's usual, lowest and
  highest yield and `cost_per_usable_unit` (latest price ÷ usual yield). When
  someone compares vendors on a trimmed item, compare these, not the raw
  price.
- **Delivered weight.** A catch-weight invoice line with a delivered weight
  is priced by that weight (13.2 lb for $121.44 = $9.2000/lb), not the
  case's nominal weight; the line's working names the invoice either way.

The rounding is fixed: a line `round(quantity × unit cost, 4)`, the total
`round(Σ lines, 4)`, per yield unit `round(total ÷ yield, 4)`, food cost
`round(per portion ÷ menu price × 100, 1)`.

## Procedure

1. **Find the recipe** — `list_rows` on `recipes` filtered by `name` (or
   take the id you were handed).
2. **Read its cost** — `recipe_cost`. For "what moved", read it twice: now, and
   `as_of` the day before the delivery in question (the alert baseline). A
   move is prices, the recipe, or both: if `recipe_line_versions` (or
   `recipe_versions`) has a row for it with `valid_to` in that window, say
   which line or setting changed, not only which price.
3. **Go down any prep line** that matters to the question — `recipe_cost` on
   its `sub_recipe_id`.
4. **Name each incomplete line** and its cause: `no quantity`, `unit
   mismatch` (the line's unit isn't the ingredient's unit / the prep's yield
   unit — recipe-entry fixes it), `cost incomplete` (the ingredient has no
   price yet — an invoice line for it will price it; or a prep under it is
   itself incomplete). An incomplete recipe has **no** total: never quote a
   partial sum as its cost.
5. **Read its held lines** — `recipe_held_lines` for the recipe. `held` > 0
   → name each one (its title says the line and what clears it) and call the
   cost **incomplete**: "$2.40 a portion, 16.0% — incomplete: 2 lines held
   (sea salt on the Dough, the honey)". The figure is what the written lines
   cost; never present it as the dish's cost, never as a food cost to act
   on, even though the database says `costed`.
6. **Report** (below).

## Rules

- **Never compute a cost by hand** and never write one: quote the database.
- **Cost basis is what was paid** on the latest invoice line for each
  ingredient (`ingredient_costs`). invoice-intake's 15% cost-move flag is the
  guard against one odd invoice spreading into every recipe — mention it when
  a dish moved because of it.
- **Say the method.** A breakdown prep's cost is always quoted with its
  method — matched (and the delivery), estimated (and the usual yield), or
  standard yield — and an estimated or standard one is called that, never
  passed off as matched.
- **Held lines make a cost incomplete.** A dish whose held count is above 0
  is reported as incomplete, with every held line named — a 3.3% food cost
  with its banana missing is not a 3.3% dish.
- **Owner's words.** Say recipe, ingredient, invoice, delivery, vendor item,
  dish, by-product, yield — never the names of tables or views.

## Report

```
Arugula Salad — $2.68 a portion, food cost 20.6% (menu $13.00)
  was $2.56 / 19.7% before US Foods delivered on 2023-07-03 (+0.9 pts)
  3 oz × $0.3359 per oz (Baby Arugula — US Foods 2023-07-03) = $1.0077
  2 fl oz × $0.4212 per fl oz (Garlic Confit prep: $16.8480 batch ÷ 40 fl oz) = $0.8424
  …
  ⚠ none over 30% · none up 2+ pts
```

A dish over a breakdown prep shows the prep's method in its line:

```
Steak Frites — $9.12 a portion, food cost 30.4% (menu $30.00)
  0.5 lb × $16.4706 per lb (Hangar Steak, Trimmed: matched, 85% yield, US Foods #4471 2026-10-28) = $8.2353
  …
```

A dish with held lines says so on its first line:

```
Brûléed Banana — $0.30 a portion so far, food cost 3.3% (menu $9.00) — incomplete: 1 line held
  ⚠ held: Banana — no price yet (it lands with the next invoice that prices it)
```

Then anything the owner should act on: held lines and the question that
clears each, alerts (food cost and yield), menu
dishes nearest the line, lines that are incomplete and what would complete
them, breakdown preps still at their standard yield (a yield log would price
them from a real delivery), and any heads-up.
