# Recipe Costing

<!-- Restaurant skill. Read-only: it reaches the restaurant's data ONLY
through the restaurant's own Bread 'n' Butter MCP (`.mcp.json`; verbs
recipe_cost · list_rows). The costing is the restaurant database's views
(supabase/restaurant/0002_costing.sql in the app repo) — this skill never
computes a cost and never writes one. -->

A recipe's cost is never stored: the database computes it on every read from
the latest invoice prices, the recipe's lines, its prep recipes and its yield.
So nothing goes stale and nothing needs re-costing — an invoice entered a
minute ago is already in every dish above it. This skill's job is to **read
the cost and explain it** in words a cook can check.

## What the database answers

| Where | What it gives |
|---|---|
| `recipe_cost` `{ recipe_id }` | the recipe's `recipe_costs` row — `total_cost`, `cost_per_yield_unit`, `food_cost_pct` (menu dishes), `status` (`costed` · `cost incomplete` · `cost incomplete: loop or too deep`), `incomplete_lines` — and every line from `recipe_line_costs` with `unit_cost`, `line_cost`, `status` and **`working`**, the line's math in words |
| `recipe_cost` `{ recipe_id, as_of: "YYYY-MM-DD" }` | the same, with only the prices known on that date and the recipe as it was that day (its lines, yield and menu price) |
| `list_rows` `recipe_line_versions` / `recipe_versions` | the recipe's history, kept by the database: each old version of a line or of the recipe's yield / menu price, with `valid_from` → `valid_to`. Read-only. Changes on the day a recipe got its first line are its starting recipe and keep no version |
| `list_rows` `recipe_costs` | every recipe at once (filter `is_prep`, sort `-food_cost_pct`) |
| `list_rows` `cost_alerts` | dishes over **30%**, or up **2+ points** since the day before the latest delivery |
| `list_rows` `ingredient_costs` | each ingredient's price per base unit, the date and the vendor it came from |

A prep line's `working` names the prep's batch cost; call `recipe_cost` on its
`sub_recipe_id` to go one level deeper.

The rounding is fixed: a line `round(quantity × unit cost, 4)`, the total
`round(Σ lines, 4)`, per yield unit `round(total ÷ yield, 4)`, food cost
`round(per portion ÷ menu price × 100, 1)`.

## Procedure

1. **Find the recipe** — `list_rows` on `recipes` filtered by `name` (or
   take the id you were handed).
2. **Read its cost** — `recipe_cost`. For "what moved", read it twice: now, and
   `as_of` the day before the delivery in question (the alert baseline). A
   move is prices, the recipe, or both: if `recipe_line_versions` (or
   `recipe_versions`) has a row for it with `valid_to` in that window, say
   which line or setting changed, not only which price.
3. **Go down any prep line** that matters to the question — `recipe_cost` on
   its `sub_recipe_id`.
4. **Name each incomplete line** and its cause: `no quantity`, `unit
   mismatch` (the line's unit isn't the ingredient's unit / the prep's yield
   unit — recipe-entry fixes it), `cost incomplete` (the ingredient has no
   price yet — an invoice line for it will price it; or a prep under it is
   itself incomplete). An incomplete recipe has **no** total: never quote a
   partial sum as its cost.
5. **Report** (below).

## Rules

- **Never compute a cost by hand** and never write one: quote the database.
- **Cost basis is what was paid** on the latest invoice line for each
  ingredient (`ingredient_costs`). invoice-intake's 15% cost-move flag is the
  guard against one odd invoice spreading into every recipe — mention it when
  a dish moved because of it.
- **Raw weights.** Proteins are costed at purchased weight until ingredients
  carry a cooking/trim yield; say so when a protein dish's food cost is quoted.
- **Owner's words.** Say recipe, ingredient, invoice, vendor item, dish — never
  the names of tables or views.

## Report

```
Arugula Salad — $2.68 a portion, food cost 20.6% (menu $13.00)
  was $2.56 / 19.7% before US Foods delivered on 2023-07-03 (+0.9 pts)
  3 oz × $0.3359 per oz (Baby Arugula — US Foods 2023-07-03) = $1.0077
  2 fl oz × $0.4212 per fl oz (Garlic Confit prep: $16.8480 batch ÷ 40 fl oz) = $0.8424
  …
  ⚠ none over 30% · none up 2+ pts
```

Then anything the owner should act on: alerts, menu dishes nearest the line,
lines that are incomplete and what would complete them, protein at raw weight.
